## Derived data (`data/*.csv`, `docs/data/*.csv`)

The tables are derivative works of public data released under the Korea Open Government License (KOGL) Type 1 (attribution) by the National Institute of Korean History (datasets 15053647 and 15053645 at data.go.kr) and of the civil-examination roster released without restriction by the Academy of Korean Studies (15052752). They may be used, modified and redistributed as long as the following attribution is kept:

> 본 저작물은 '교육부 국사편찬위원회'에서 2022년 작성하여 공공누리 제1유형으로 개방한 '조선왕조실록 정보_실록원문'·'조선왕조실록 정보_부가정보 인물 데이터'를 이용하였으며, 해당 저작물은 '공공데이터포털(https://www.data.go.kr/data/15053647/fileData.do · https://www.data.go.kr/data/15053645/fileData.do)'에서 무료로 다운받으실 수 있습니다. 성관 보충에는 '한국학중앙연구원'이 공공데이터포털(https://www.data.go.kr/data/15052752/fileData.do)에 개방한 '조선조문과급제자'를 이용하였습니다.

Astronomical reference tables were computed from the JPL DE406 ephemeris (NASA/JPL, public domain) and cross-checked against Espenak & Meeus, *Five Millennium Canon of Solar Eclipses* (NASA/TP-2006-214141), whose reproduction is freely granted with credit.

No text of the Veritable Records is included. The Gojong and Sunjong records (KOGL Type 4) are not used.
