Sillok Metrics

The Veritable Records of the Joseon Dynasty counted article by article, with a reliability card on every number

Scope: the 27 reigns from Taejo to Sunjong (1392–1910). The Gojong and Sunjong records are released under KOGL Type 4 (no derivatives, non-commercial), so they enter only counts that neither alter nor quote the text (articles, characters, NIKH classification, keyword hits); titles, text excerpts and the astronomical checks exclude them, and in the published coding tables their rows keep the judgment but have their title, quote and summary blanked. The 25 reigns Taejo–Cheoljong are Type 1 (attribution).

Characters per year 1392–1910 (principal editions); bands mark reigns.

Key points

1. The Sillok is more uneven than one might expect: characters per reign-year fall from around 100,000 (Sejong–Sukjong) to the low ten-thousands (Heonjong, Cheoljong).
2. Of eclipse observation reports, 65% match an eclipse visible from Seoul and 95% one somewhere on Earth; if article types are ignored, the rate is 24%.
3. Daytime sightings of Venus on 2,937 days reproduce the planet's synodic period to within 0.04%: observation records are observations.
4. The top-10 clan share among first appointees to high office rose from 0.28 to 0.45 — the concentration shows up in the numbers.
5. The Sejong peak in death-penalty articles is sentencing paperwork; the Sillok is not a source of penal statistics.

The weather cell of the Seungjeongwon Ilgi

The 102,397 day-level weather cells of the Seungjeongwon Ilgi, decomposed with a token rule. The cell was first digitized in full by Lyu et al. (2024, Geoscience Data Journal, Zenodo CC BY 4.0) for 1623–1910, and Lee, Shin & Hong (2024, Scientific Data, Zenodo CC BY 4.0) published its categories for 1778–1907 together with the rain-gauge series. This chapter adds the day-by-day agreement with those two datasets and the list of differences, the degree to which a bare '晴' was entered as routine, the overlap with the rain gauge and with modern observations, and the structure of the gaps.
