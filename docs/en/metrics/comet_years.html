Comet and guest-star articles

Articles mentioning comets, broom stars or guest stars, by year, with the precision of 'actual observation report' measured on a coded sample.

1,417articles · precision 0.68
Key point

Read comet article counts multiplied by the 68% precision; one in three is a memorial or debate citing a comet. The Halley-year peaks survive the correction.

Formula
keyword article count; precision = observation reports in sample / sample
How to read it
Peaks are expected in Halley apparition years (1456, 1531, 1607, 1682, 1759, 1835).
Caveats
'彗' also appears figuratively and in names, so precision is below 1.
