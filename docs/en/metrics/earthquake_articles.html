Earthquake articles

'地震' articles by year and province, with sample precision.

1,768articles · precision 0.76
Key point

Of earthquake articles, 76% are actual reports, so they are usable as a source for historical seismicity catalogs. Articles without a province are assigned to Seoul, so regional comparisons need care.

Formula
Keyword count; province from a province name in the article, otherwise Seoul (observatory report).
How to read it
Sillok earthquake articles are a main source of the historical seismicity catalog (Lee & Yang 2006).
Caveats
Assigning articles without a province name to Seoul can misplace provincial reports.
Reliability card
sampledSampled precision — share of keyword hits that mean what the metric says, measured on a coded stratified sample

Precision 76.3% (Wilson 95% 66.8%–83.8%) on a coded stratified sample of 93 articles (10 per 50-year period, principal editions). The 5 articles left undecided were counted as 'no'. The codebook and all judgments can be downloaded from the Data page.
