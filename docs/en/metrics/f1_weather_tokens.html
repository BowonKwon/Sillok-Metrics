The weather cell of the Seungjeongwon Ilgishare

Token shares of the weather cell and agreement with two datasets

Each cell decomposed with the pre-registered token rule R1 into day-level flags (clear-only, cloudy, rain, snow, mixed), by reign and lunar month, and the same-day agreement with the Lyu et al. 2024 dummies and the Lee et al. 2024 category.

75.1%of weather days carry only '晴'
Key point

Three quarters of the days carry nothing but '晴' (76,308 with the bare character, 181 with a spelling variant such as 〈晴〉 or 朝晴). This site's rule-based decomposition agrees with the Lyu et al. 2024 dummies on 99.5–100.0% of days and with the Lee et al. 2024 category on 97.4–99.97%; the remaining 776 and 1,258 differences are published as lists. These are entries in a secretariat form, not Seoul's weather.

Formula
Class tokens (晴 陰 雨 雪 雹 霧 雷 風 霜) plus time and degree modifiers decompose all 896 distinct strings with zero residue; rain_any etc. are true if present anywhere in the day (precip_any = rain, snow or hail). Denominators are weather-bearing day nodes (101,793; a date with two nodes counts twice). Agreement = days with the same flag / days in both (Lyu joined on the NIKH day ID, 102,671 days; Lee on the Gregorian date, 45,674 of the 47,240 matched days that carry category 1, 3, 7 or 8 — 1,566 missing or no-document days excluded; of the four dates that appear twice in Lee's file, the row with a gauge value is kept).
How to read it
The shares describe entry practice. Agreement in the 99% range means the decomposition is stable, not that the weather was right. The difference lists (f_compare_*_diff) carry the original strings and take no side.
Caveats
Lyu et al. 2024 digitized the same cell first. What is new here is the published rule and the three-way check; the rule was not adjusted after seeing the results.
External check
Lyu et al. 2024: 102,689 rows in the Zenodo release (doi:10.5281/zenodo.8382243, CC BY 4.0), while the paper (§6) states 100,620 days with weather records; Lee et al. 2024 (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0), 47,480 days (Methods, quality assurance).
Reliability card
externalExternal match — agreement with other datasets and observations

Same NIKH day ID as Lyu et al. 2024 on 102,671 days: clear 99.53%, cloudy 99.83%, rain 99.90%, snow 99.98% agreement; identical strings on 98.3%, identical Gregorian date 99.94%.

Lee et al. 2024, joined on the Gregorian date, 47,240 days (1778–1907): clear 97.4%, rain 99.83%, snow 99.97%. Lee's single category per day disagrees on mixed days by construction.
