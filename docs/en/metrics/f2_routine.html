The weather cell of the Seungjeongwon Ilgishare

Routine-entry index — the bare 晴 and the rain gauge

By reign: share of days with '晴' alone, longest and mean run of such days, share of solar-July (monsoon) days with rain recorded; and the share of rain-gauge days (Lee et al. 2024) on which the cell says 雨.

72%of 8,364 gauge rain days carry 雨 (false rain 1.7%)
Key point

Only 72% of days with measured rain carry 雨 in the cell, while 雨 appears on 1.7% of days without a measurement. Rain was omitted but not invented — the cell was a form with 'clear' as its default.

Formula
Gauge recall = rain_any days among days with gauge depth > 0. False rain = rain_any days among days with no or zero gauge record. Wilson 95% intervals by 50-year period.
How to read it
Recall well below 1 with false rain near 0 is a form that omits but does not invent. Long 晴 runs and few July rain days mark reigns whose cell is furthest from observation.
Caveats
The gauge record has its own gaps (Lee et al. 2024 flag columns) and its measurement day does not coincide exactly with the diary day. The comparison is limited to 1778–1907.
External check
Lee, Shin & Hong 2024: Seoul rain-gauge daily series 1778–1907.
Reliability card
