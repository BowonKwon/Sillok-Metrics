The weather cell of the Seungjeongwon Ilgishare

Overlap with modern observations, 1907–1910

On the 1,052 days from the start of KMA Seoul (108) daily data (1907-10-01) to the diary's last day (1910-08-29): agreement between observed rain, snow and cloud cover and the cell's flags.

21%of 403 observed rain days carry 雨, 1907–1910
Key point

On the 1,052 days overlapping KMA observations (1907–1910) only 21% of observed rain days carry 雨, and only 24% of '晴'-only days have mean cloud cover ≤ 2.5 tenths. In its last years the cell cannot be read as an observation record.

Formula
Observed rain = daily precipitation > 0 or '비' in the phenomena field; snow = '눈' in phenomena (snow-depth columns are empty in these years); clear = mean cloud cover ≤ 2.5 tenths, cloudy ≥ 7.5. Agreement, recall and precision with Wilson 95% intervals.
How to read it
Low agreement does not mean the station was wrong; it means the cell had stopped being an observation by the last years of the Korean Empire. Do not use the cell of this period as climate data.
Caveats
1,052 days from one period cannot be generalised backwards. The station (Yeonhwabang) and the secretariat (near Deoksugung) are a few kilometres apart.
External check
