Planet articles against the computed sky — Venus elongation and 犯 separations

(1) Distribution of Venus's elongation on 'Venus seen by day' dates per source against random dates. (2) For '月犯X / X犯Y' articles whose two bodies are in the name dictionary, the minimum angular separation that night.

43°median Venus elongation, Goryeosa (random: 30°)
Key point

On 'Venus seen by day' dates the planet's elongation has a median near 43° in all three sources against 31° on random dates, and for the '月犯X / X犯Y' pairs (Goryeosa 454 pairs: 285 Moon, 169 planet) the minimum separation that night has a median of 2.8° (2.2° with a ±1-day window) against 91° at random. Only about four in ten fall within 1.5°, so 犯 should not be read as a modern close approach.

Formula
(1) Median elongation at local noon and shares ≥30°/≥40°; control: 2,000 random days of the same span. (2) Minimum separation during the night of the recorded date (18 h to 06 h local mean time, 30-min steps), median and shares ≤1.5°/≤5°, also with a ±1-day window; control: the same pairs on 400 random dates. Per-article values (g3_venus_articles, g3_appulse_articles) and the control draws (g3_*_control) are published.
How to read it
Distributions that separate from random mean the dates reflect the real sky. Absolute separations depend on which star a Chinese name denotes (dictionary of 16 star names — 15 positions, as names such as 房 and 鉤鈐 share one — and 6 planet names for 5 planets) and are indicative.
Caveats
Only pairs resolved by the dictionary were computed (Goryeosa 454, Ilgi 156; none for the Sillok, whose 1.0 keyword contexts are too short). Asterism names are represented by one star. Lee 2023's mean of 1.2° for 犯 in the Chunbang Ilgi comes from a precisely dated source and is not directly comparable.
External check
Lee 2023 (JKAS 56): Chunbang Ilgi, 1,443 days with astronomical records out of 2,799, mean separation 1.2° for 犯 and 1.0° for 食.
