Same-event reporting between sources

Share of the same events (solar and lunar observation days, comet keyword days) recorded in both (1) the Goryeosa treatise and the Jeolyo, (2) the Sillok and the Ilgi (1623–1863), in both directions.

32%of 37 Sillok eclipse days also in the Ilgi, 1623–1863
Key point

Of Goryeosa eclipse observation days, 91% recur in the Jeolyo, but only 32% of Sillok eclipse observation days of 1623–1863 recur in the Ilgi, while 91% of comet keyword days do. Overlap differs by phenomenon; as the two lists differ in selection, survival and type coding, the figure alone does not settle whether one compilation copied the other.

Formula
Of one source's dates, the share found in the other within ±1 day. Eclipses use observation days classified by type; comets use keyword days.
How to read it
The Jeolyo abridges the Goryeosa, so eclipses nearly all recur. Sillok and Ilgi overlap little on eclipse observation days and much on comet keyword days. The two lists differ in selection, survival and type coding, so the overlap is not read as evidence for or against copying.
Caveats
The Ilgi survives only from Injo, hence the cut to 1623-01-01 – 1863-12-31 (Gregorian); Sillok dates use the 1.0 conversion, Ilgi dates the ganji-corrected conversion.
