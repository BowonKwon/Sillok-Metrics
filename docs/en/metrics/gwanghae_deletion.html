Deletions in the Gwanghaegun diary

Share of characters marked as deleted in the second draft, and the drop in articles and characters from the second to the final draft.

16.4%second-draft characters marked deleted
Key point

Splits NIKH's 'reduced by a third' into three measurable things — 15% of articles, 18% of characters, 16% of characters marked — which measure different things and must be cited as such.

Formula
characters inside deletion marks / characters of the second draft; 1 − final/second for articles and for characters
How to read it
Articles fell by 15%; the character drop and the share of marked deletions are in the table. The three differ because deletions were made within articles as well as of whole articles.
Caveats
Deletion marks follow NIKH's proofreading markup. Text added in the final draft is not captured.
