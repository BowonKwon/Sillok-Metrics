Volume and density of the recordcharacters/year

Characters per reign-year

Characters divided by the length of the reign in years — the density of the record.

205,623highest: Injong, per year
Key point

Turns the commonplace that the record thins toward the end into a number. The tenfold drop after Jeongjo should be read as a change in recording practice, not as a quieter century.

Formula
Characters / reign length in years, where years = (JDN of last dated article − JDN of first + 1) / 365.2422 — the same rule before and after the 1896 switch to the solar calendar. Reign months = calendar months touched by that span (lunar months incl. leap months to Dec 1895, solar months from Jan 1896).
How to read it
Sejong, Seongjong, Jungjong, Seonjo and Sukjong exceed 100,000 a year; after Jeongjo the density collapses to 10–15 thousand under Heonjong and Cheoljong.
Caveats
Reign length is measured from the first to the last dated article, which may differ slightly from the dates of accession and death.
