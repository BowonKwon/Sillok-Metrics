The weather cell of the Seungjeongwon Ilgi

The 102,397 day-level weather cells of the Seungjeongwon Ilgi, decomposed with a token rule. The cell was first digitized in full by Lyu et al. (2024, Geoscience Data Journal, Zenodo CC BY 4.0) for 1623–1910, and Lee, Shin & Hong (2024, Scientific Data, Zenodo CC BY 4.0) published its categories for 1778–1907 together with the rain-gauge series. This chapter adds the day-by-day agreement with those two datasets and the list of differences, the degree to which a bare '晴' was entered as routine, the overlap with the rain gauge and with modern observations, and the structure of the gaps.
