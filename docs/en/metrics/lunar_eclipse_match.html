Lunar-eclipse articles matched by computation

Share of '月食' articles whose date lies within ±1 day of a computed umbral lunar eclipse with the Moon above the Seoul horizon.

46.5%of 359 articles, untyped
Key point

Only 47% of all lunar-eclipse articles match a visible eclipse within ±1 day. Untyped, hence a lower bound to be redone after typing in 1.1; do not use the article count as an observation count yet.

Formula
matched / lunar-eclipse articles; penumbral eclipses excluded.
How to read it
Lunar eclipses are seen from the whole night hemisphere, so a higher match rate than for solar eclipses is expected.
Caveats
For eclipses after midnight the previous date is also accepted, following the period's practice of not changing the date.
Reliability card
externalExternal match — compared with an astronomical computation

Of 359 '月食' articles (not typed), 46.5% match within ±1 day (95% 41.4%–51.7%). Discussion and retrospective articles are mixed in, so this is a lower bound; typing is planned for 1.1.
