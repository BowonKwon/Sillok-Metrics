Characters

Number of Chinese characters in the article text (including notes and historians' comments; titles and bibliographic data excluded).

52.5Mcharacters, 33 editions
Key point

The only complete count on which any statement about the Sillok's size can rest: 52.5 million characters across all 33 editions, 44.6 million in the principal editions of Taejo–Cheoljong alone.

Formula
Count of CJK unified ideographs and extensions; Hangul (modern editorial notes) counted separately.
How to read it
About 52.5 million across the 33 editions, 44.6 million in the principal editions of Taejo–Cheoljong. Jungjong is the longest edition (5.9 million); Jeongjo has many characters for its article count — its articles are long.
Caveats
Punctuation and spaces are not counted. Both characters of an editorial correction '(甲)〔乙〕' are counted.
External check
Same counting method as the 2026-09 conversion check (XML <text> characters vs converted text, 743 files identical).
