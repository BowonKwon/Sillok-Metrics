Characters

Number of Chinese characters in the article text (including notes and historians' comments; titles and bibliographic data excluded).

52.5Mcharacters, 33 editions
Key point

The only complete count behind any statement about the Sillok's size: 52.5 million characters across all 33 editions, 44.6 million in the principal editions of Taejo–Cheoljong alone.

Formula
count of CJK unified ideographs and extensions; Hangul (modern editorial notes) counted separately.
How to read it
About 49.5 million in all. Jungjong is the longest edition (5.9 million); Jeongjo has many characters for its article count — its articles are long.
Caveats
Punctuation and spaces are not counted. Both characters of an editorial correction '(甲)〔乙〕' are counted.
